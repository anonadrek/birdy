## 1. Press Release

### English (~250 words)

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE — 17 June 2026**

**Headline: The anti-surveillance bird app: Birdy identifies birds fully on-device — nothing ever leaves your phone**

A Swedish solo developer has launched **Birdy**, a bird-identification app for Android that runs its AI entirely on the device. No account. No ads. No tracking. No cloud. The promise is blunt: almost nothing is collected, and your data stays on your phone.

While most identification apps phone home with photos, audio, and location, Birdy does the opposite. Photo and bird-song recognition happen locally, so the app works in the field with no signal — and there is no server quietly building a profile of where you walk or what you watch.

Birdy covers **839 European species** and wraps the experience in a hand-drawn **field-journal aesthetic**: paper textures, ink headlines, and collectible wax-seal stamps. Every sighting becomes a page you actually own — saved to a private diary, never uploaded. A built-in encyclopedia, a personal on-device finds map, and gentle gamification round out the experience.

"I wanted a bird app that respects you," says the developer, Albin. "Identification is the easy part. The hard part is building something that doesn't surveil the person using it. Birdy keeps every find on your phone, where it belongs — a digital field journal you own, not a data pipeline you feed."

Birdy is **free** for everyone, with premium features currently unlocked during launch. Available now on Google Play in Sweden, the wider Nordics, and across Europe, localized in Swedish and English.

**Website:** birdy.community · **Contact:** albin@abrahamssons.se

## 2. Factsheet

**What it is**
- Birdy is a privacy-first bird-identification app: photo ID + bird-song (audio) ID, plus a field-journal diary, an encyclopedia, and a private finds map.

**Platform**
- Android (Google Play). iOS planned for a later release. Built on Kotlin Multiplatform + Compose Multiplatform.

**Price**
- Free for everyone. Premium tier exists (PDF export, season statistics, extra collectible stamps) but is currently unlocked free during launch. No ads in any version.

**Privacy facts**
- AI runs fully on-device — photos and audio are never uploaded.
- No account required.
- No ads, no third-party trackers, no analytics SDK.
- Works fully offline, in the field, with no signal.
- The personal finds map and diary live only on your phone; location capture is opt-in.
- Privacy promise: almost nothing collected, data stays on the phone.

**Species coverage**
- 839 European species at launch (Nordics/EU focus).

**Key features**
- On-device photo identification.
- On-device bird-song / audio identification.
- Field Journal diary — every sighting saved as a page you own.
- Encyclopedia of all 839 species.
- Collectible wax-seal "stamp" gamification.
- Private, on-device finds map.
- Localized in Swedish and English.

**Availability**
- Live now in 10 countries — Sweden, wider Nordics, and across Europe.

**Developer**
- Albin — independent solo developer, Sweden.

**Links**
- Website: https://birdy.community
- Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=se.birdy.android
- Privacy / Terms / Data safety: https://birdy.community/legal/

**Contact**
- albin@abrahamssons.se
