## Captions

Every post is in English. The caption gives the bird's Swedish name in brackets after the English name and adds a couple of Swedish hashtags to the English ones, for example:

```text
The Eurasian Blue Tit (Blåmes) is a tit with a pale blue crown and upperparts and pale yellow underparts.

#birds #birdwatching #birdsong #birding #birdy #EurasianBlueTit #fåglar #fågelskådning
```
