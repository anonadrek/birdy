Where we stand

Where things stand, honestly.

Birdy is a small, independent app made by AlbIT AB in Solna, Sweden. No ads, no tracking, no account. Your field journal stays on your phone.

When Birdy launched in June, Premium was open to everyone. From Birdy 1.3, Premium is bought in Google Play. Premium is what lets us keep building Birdy, and the next big step is the iPhone app.

Early field member

Here from the start?

Then Premium is already yours. If you started using Birdy before 17 October 2026, 00:00 Swedish time, you get Premium free, forever. A thank-you for being here while Birdy was new.

You don't need to do anything. Update the app, and the thank-you screen greets you the next time you open it.

The app works this out on your phone and sends nothing anywhere. The proof travels with Google's backup if you change phones.

Buy

How to get Premium.

  1. Get Birdy from Google Play.
  2. Open Premium in the app, from Lifelist or Settings.
  3. Choose a year or lifetime and pay in Google Play.
Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel the yearly plan in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions. Premium stays until the end of the year you paid for.

What happens to my finds if I stop paying?

Nothing. The field journal is free and your finds stay on your phone. Only the Premium parts close.

Changing phones?

The purchase belongs to your Google account and moves with you to the new phone.

Does it carry over to iPhone?

No. Birdy has no accounts, so a Google Play purchase applies to the Android app. The iPhone app will have its own purchases in the App Store.
