B's letter is lovely, but only if you'd write it yourself

Open questions

  1. The early-user wording. The app recognises early users from proof: the install time 1.2 saved, or the network time at the first start on Android 13 or newer. A brand new install on Android 12 or older on 15 or 16 Oct gets no proof, and neither does someone who installed 1.2 but never opened it. The mockups therefore say "började använda Birdy före..." (started using). Keep that, or should the code also cover old Android? Until it does, don't use "ladda ned före 17 oktober" as an ad hook.
  2. "För alltid". The thank-you screen says "För alltid" in the headline and "så länge du har appen" in the text. The privacy policy describes the early-user rule; the terms don't mention it. Keep "för alltid" on the web, and add a line to the terms?
  3. iPhone. The FAQ says a Google Play purchase does not carry over to the iPhone app, because Birdy has no accounts. True by design; fine to say now?
  4. Lift C's bird. Same bird as the app every day (port the app's picker to the site build)? And when the day's bird has no published species page yet, or only a CC BY photo: show it without a link, or pick from published species with CC0/PD photos only?
  5. Premium B is written in your name. Edit freely if you pick it.
  6. The PDF. In the emulator export the species pages show empty photo boxes and very tight rows, so the mockups only show the cover, "2026 i siffror" and the stamps page. Worth a look before people print it.
  7. Where Premium lives in the app. "Öppna Premium i appen, under Mina arter eller Inställningar": I used the Mina arter entry (Säsongsstatistik, PREMIUM) on the emulator, and the Settings card for free users from the 1.3 screenshots. Right?
  8. Monthly. The 49 kr monthly plan comes in 1.3.1, so the pages say "ett år eller för alltid" now and get a third card then. OK?

Checked against

  • Premium contents: terms §5 on release/1.3.0 (map, PDF, season statistics, seven badges) and the app's own Premium screen.
  • Audio always free: terms §4 and the privacy policy (BirdNET, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).
  • Prices: 199 kr a year, 499 kr once, incl. VAT, as set up in Play Console; the app always shows Play's price.
  • Cutoff: 17 Oct 2026 00.00 Stockholm, the build constant for vC130.
  • No ads or tracking, journal stays on the phone: privacy policy §1 and §4. No "offline" and no accuracy claims anywhere, and no dashes in the copy.
  • Photos: skäggmes, rook, bläsand, vitkindad gås and sillgrissla are CC0 or public domain, named on the pages. Bird photos inside app screens are the app's own.
