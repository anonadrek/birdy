Birdy 1.3.1 · Google Play

The store images in the Flock look

Six screenshots and a feature graphic per language, moved from Espresso to the website’s Flock look so the store matches birdy.community before 1.3.1 is sent to Google. Same captions, same app captures. Nothing is uploaded to Play Console.

What changed

  • Peach paper lit like the home page instead of espresso; espresso headlines, the handwritten word in rust, the note in a quieter brown.
  • The phone is whole and floats with a soft shadow; Espresso cut it off at the bottom.
  • A quiet stream of the flock’s small birds passes behind each phone and joins from image to image. No arrows, lines or notes on top.
  • Birdy’s bird made of the 839 birds appears only on the feature graphic, with the slogan.

Files

  • docs/play-store/store-assets/1.3.1-flock/sv/ and /en/, the same file names as 1.3.0.
  • Screenshots 1080 × 1920 PNG, 0.3 to 1.4 MB. Feature graphic 1024 × 500 PNG, 0.2 MB. RGB without alpha.
  • Rendered by variant F in website/tools/store-assets/1.3/; the Espresso variant renders byte for byte as before.

Worth knowing

Screenshot 01 is the 1.3.0 capture. Its small line under “Tre sätt att fånga” still has a dash between “Kamera, foto eller läte” and “arten dyker upp i din fältbok” (the same in English), where 1.3.1 now has a colon. A new capture from the 1.3.1 build would match the app exactly.

The map screenshot is still left out, as in 1.3.0: it is added with the same tool once the MapTiler style is in the app.
