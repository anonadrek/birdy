What the set keeps

Bird photos untouched

No tint, tape or line over any bird photo. Only CC0 or public domain photos appear, all of them inside the app's own screenshots.

Play's format

PNG without an alpha channel, exactly 1080 × 1920 and 1024 × 500, each under 3 MB. The render script refuses anything else.

Honest words

No dashes in our text, no "offline", no "free", no ranking or accuracy claims.
