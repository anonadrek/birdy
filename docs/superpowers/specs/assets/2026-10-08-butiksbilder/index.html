Store images for 1.3.0

Three looks for the Google Play listing, all built from the same five 1.3.0 screenshots and the website's colours and handwriting. Pick one set of screenshots and one feature graphic at the bottom. The chosen set is rendered as PNG and goes up together with vC130.

tap an image to see it large

What every variant keeps

Bird photos untouched

No tint, tape or arrow over any bird photo. Only CC0 or public domain photos appear: the screenshots, plus one CC0 bearded reedling in feature graphic B.

Play's format

1080 × 1920 screenshots and a 1024 × 500 feature graphic, PNG without alpha. The render script checks every file.

Honest words

No dashes, no "offline", no "free", no ranking or accuracy claims. Headlines reuse the website's tone, starting with "Se. Lyssna. Spara."

The map waits

Image 6 (the map) is left out of every set: it still shows the old sepia look. It is retaken next week with your own MapTiler style.
