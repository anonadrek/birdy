Birdy 1.3.1 · Märken 1b

Two screens, one stamp.

You asked for the weekly Fältrapport and the Troférum to point at each other. Here are three ways to do it, drawn on the screens as they will look in 1.3.1, with my pick at the end. None of them adds a screen.

The Troférum is drawn as Märken 1b's brass shelves, one per month, and a stamp's card as its certificate. The links work on today's layout too. In-app text is Swedish; the sketches show week 41 (5 to 11 October), when Månads-rytm was won.

A

Stämpeln som bro

Recommended

The stamp is the bridge. Tap it in the report to find it on its shelf; tap its week on the shelf to read that week's report.

What happens

  1. 1The Ny stämpel card ends in a line, Se den i troférummet. It opens the Troférum at the stamp's shelf and rings the stamp for a moment.
  2. 2Every stamp on the shelves gets its week next to the date. Tapping v. 41 opens that week's Fältrapport, also for weeks long past.

Edge cases

No new stamp this week
The card keeps Ingen ny stämpel den här veckan. and names the stamp closest to unlocking, which opens the Troférum. Only on the current week's report: an old report would show today's progress.

Won before weekly reports existed
Works. A report is built from your saved finds when you open it, so v. 23 on Nybörjare, won 2 June before Birdy had weekly reports, opens a full week 23. A week from another year shows the year: v. 41, 2025.
Won in a week with no other finds
A stamp's week is when the app handed it out, the same rule the report uses to list it, so the link always lands on a report that shows the stamp. Fältmedlem arrives when Premium turns on and can fall in an empty week; that report reads En lugn vecka. with the stamp card, as it does today.

Work: medium. The Troférum learns which stamp to ring, the report's stamp card becomes a button, each stamp on the shelves gets a week tag with a full-size touch area (the shelves grow a little), and the report needs badge progress for Närmast.

B

Veckans rad i troférummet

Each screen borrows a band from the other: the Troférum shows this week, the report shows the stamps in reach.

What happens

  1. 1A Den här veckan card above the shelves shows the week's new stamps and opens the current Fältrapport.
  2. 2The report's stamp card ends in Till troférummet. In a week without a stamp, the two stamps closest to unlocking take the card's place.

C

Ett stämpelkort

One card holds both ways. Tap a stamp anywhere and its certificate, which Märken 1b builds anyway, offers the week's report and the Troférum.

What happens

  1. 1The Ny stämpel card in the report opens the stamp's certificate, as a stamp on the shelves or on Märken already does.
  2. 2Vecka 41 · Läs veckans uppslag opens the report for the week the stamp was won.
  3. 3Se alla i troférummet opens the shelves. The row for the screen you came from is hidden.

Edge cases

No new stamp this week
Nothing to tap, so the two screens don't meet that week, unless the empty card gets a plain Till troférummet.

Till troférummet (optional)

Won before weekly reports existed
Works as in A: the week row rebuilds that week's report from your saved finds.
Won in a week with no other finds
As in A: the report opens as En lugn vecka. with the stamp.

It also retires an old oddity: today the card says Nytt märke! even for a stamp won in June.

Work: small. Two rows on the certificate and a tappable stamp card in the report. The cost: one more tap, and the links stay out of sight until you open a stamp.

A

My pick

A, Stämpeln som bro.

It is the only option where you see the link on both screens without opening anything, and the link is information in itself: each stamp's week, in the unit the report already speaks. It adds no screen and leans on what exists. The report can already open any past week, and on Märken 1b's shelves every stamp has a place to be ringed.

It is the most work of the three, but each piece is small. If you want the smallest change, take C. A and C share their plumbing, so the certificate can get C's week row later for almost nothing.

For the plan

What the code already gives us

  • Any past week opens. The report's route takes an ISO week, AppRoute.WeeklyRecap(week) (AppRoute.kt:90), and the builder rebuilds that week from saved finds with the streak as it stood then (WeeklyRecapBuilder.kt:61). A and C link to old weeks for free.
  • A stamp's week is its unlock time. The report lists stamps unlocked in its week (RecapViewModel.kt:71). The unlock time is when the app hands the stamp out (RecalculateBadgesUseCase.kt:30), also after an app update or when Premium turns on.
  • The Troférum route takes nothing. data object TrophyRoom (AppRoute.kt:44) needs the stamp to ring for A; one call site (AppScaffold.kt:508) and one test (VisibleBackTest.kt:576).
  • Today's Troférum shows only the five newest stamps plus the red-listed ones (BadgesViewModel.kt:86, TrophyShowcase.kt:27). Ringing an older stamp needs Märken 1b's shelves; on today's layout A opens the stamp's card instead.
  • Progress is only computed for Märken (BadgesViewModel.kt:135), so A's Närmast and B's band need it in the report too. It counts species, families or weeks, not finds: the line reads 18 av 20 familjer (the existing badge_progress_counted), not "2 fynd kvar".
  • The report's stamp card is not tappable yet (RecapEntries.kt:171), and the stamp sheet says Nytt märke! for every stamp, old or new (UnlockBottomSheet.kt:165).
  • Two tabs meet. The report belongs to Mina arter, the Troférum to Märken (BottomNavBar.kt:132, :138). A link should open on top of the current screen so Back returns, and step back instead of stacking a loop when its target is already below.
