See the song

The first week, Fri 9 to Thu 15 Oct

Seven videos, one a day at 08:00 on Facebook, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, plus three reserves. Turn the sound on: the ring answers the bird, settles, one breath of stillness, then the photo and the name. The poster of each video is its cover, which never shows the bird.

  • page has passed its checks
  • page waits for a decision in the exception sheet

The week

Songbirds alternate with other birds, and the pages most likely to be live come first. If a page is not live on its morning, swap in a reserve. Captions link to birdy.community until the page is live; rerun the captions that morning and the link becomes the species page.
