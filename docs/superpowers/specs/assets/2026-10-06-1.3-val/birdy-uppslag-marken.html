Birdy 1.3.0 · design choices · 7 October 2026

Recap, badges and four more, before 1.3.0.

You asked for a Veckans uppslag worth opening, a badge image we're allowed to use, a lift for the whole badge section, the PDF export in 1.3 colours, a Premium nudge after a few saved birds, and a bolder intro. Each section shows the screen as it ships in vC129 today, then options in the app's own palette and type, with what changes, why it reads better and what it costs.

Example data in every phone: a free user called Albin with 31 species and 74 finds since 2 June 2026, 8 of 34 badges, and week 41 (5 to 11 October) with 7 finds, 3 new species and a 4-week streak. Bird photos stand in for the user's own photos. Not your real journal.

Effort: S is about a day including tests, SV + EN strings and new screenshots. M is two to three days. L is a week or more. Go-live is no later than 14 October, so anything for 1.3.0 has to be built and tested by about 12 October, on top of yesterday's picks (the year ring and Dagens fågel B).

House rules kept: no tint on bird photos (text goes below the photo, or on a neutral dark shade), one italic rust accent word per headline, Ljud-ID always free, prices only from Google Play.

A Veckans uppslag

The weekly recap: a spread you want to open

Today the recap is a headline, three handwritten lines and then every find of the week as a tall stacked plate, so a busy week becomes a long scroll of photos, and an audio find without a photo shows a blank plate. The data behind it is good: finds per day, new species, streak, change from last week and the week's new stamps. The options below spend that data better. Nothing here needs new data except where a note says so.

NowvC129

As it ships today

  • The numbers are a Caveat sentence, so 2 fynd, 1 ny art and the streak read as one line instead of three facts.
  • Each find is a full-width plate. Seven finds is about four screens of photos; the week's best find has no special place.
  • The entry card on Mina arter is the only way in, plus the Sunday notification.
Uppslag 1S1.3.0

The journal header and the week strip

  • What changes: the three numbers move into the same header row as Mina arter, a Monday to Sunday strip shows which days you were out, new species get their place in the life list (№ 31), the week's stamp is a real seal, and all finds become a four-column grid that opens each find.
  • Why: a busy week fits on one screen and a quiet one still looks finished. Audio finds get the species plate photo instead of an empty frame.
  • Data: all there today. The life-list number is the order of first sightings.

C Märken och troférum

Lift the badges: a stamp book, not a dot grid

Four screens make up the badge experience: the Märken tab, the sheet you get when you tap a stamp, Troférummet, and the unlock moment after a save. Today they share the 1.3 look but not a story: 34 empty dashed circles fill most of the Märken tab, earned stamps are all the same rust disc, and the sheet does not say which find earned the stamp. Two options, each drawn across all four screens.

NowvC129

Märken, the stamp sheet and Troférummet today

  • Märken: the stamp track draws all 34 badges as 52 dp dashed circles, about two screens of empty rings before the real list begins (Senast upptäckta, Skådar-milstolpar, Vanor, Premium). The one earned stamp shows a tiny Caveat "1".
  • Sheet: stamp, name, category, description, date. It says "Nytt märke!" even when you open an old one, and nothing about the find behind it.
  • Troférum: one big brass seal and "Nära att låsa upp". With few stamps the room looks empty; with many there is no order.
  • Premium teasers are five "★" circles without names, so a free user can't see what they would hunt.
  • Name clash: two badges are both called "Året runt" (all four seasons, and 12 months in a row). Both options rename the season one to "Fyra årstider".
Märken 1M1a, the Märken tab: 1.3.0 if picked this week1b, the rest: 1.3.1

The stamp page: sections, inks and "inom räckhåll"

  • Märken: the 34-ring track becomes one thin ink bar (8 of 34). An "Inom räckhåll" card shows the two closest badges and what is missing, then sections by theme, each in its own ink: Livslistan (ink), Familjer och bredd (moss), Rödlistade (rust), Säsong, ljud och dagens fågel (navy), Vanor (umber), Premium (brass).
  • Progress is drawn as an inked arc around the seal, with "3 kvar" under it. Locked badges keep their dashed ring but show their number and target.
  • Premium teasers show all seven by name with a lock, plus one "Se Premium" button. Visible to free users, never pushy.
  • Sheet as a certificate: "Intyg", the date, the find that earned it with its photo, and the next level. "Dela intyget" shares it as an image.
  • Troférum as a cabinet: earned stamps stand on brass shelves, one shelf per month, with a year tab once you have stamps from two years.
  • Unlock: one stamp slam (scale and turn, an ink ring, a haptic tick) instead of the looping pulse, then name, line, date and the next level. Reduced motion: a plain fade.
  • Accessibility: every seal keeps its spoken label ("Familjespanare, 18 av 20 klart"); inks are never the only cue (section titles and icons carry it); every ink reads at 5.5:1 or better on paper, and the cream numbers on filled seals at 5.9:1 or better; at 1.5× text the grid drops from five to four columns.
  • New data: the certificate's "fyndet som gav stämpeln". Unlocks only store badge and time today. Going forward we store the observation id at unlock (one nullable column); for older stamps the sheet shows the latest find saved before the unlock, or hides the row for backfilled stamps.
Märken 2LLater (1.4)

The field passport: rubber stamps on ruled pages

  • Märken becomes a passport with six tabbed pages (Fältet, Säsong, Ljud, Rödlistat, Dagens fågel, Premium). Earned badges are inked rubber stamps, slightly rotated, with the badge name and date around the ring. Empty slots are printed dotted circles that say what they need.
  • Tapping a stamp opens it like a visa entry: date, find, place, status, next level.
  • Troférum becomes "Året i stämplar", a month-by-month timeline with a year switcher.
  • Unlock: the passport page slides up and the stamp presses down with a thud and an ink texture; the date prints last.
  • Why later: it is the more memorable version, but every screen is new, the ink texture needs a shader or a pre-rendered mask per stamp, and six pages need their own empty states and TalkBack order. Märken 1 gets most of the clarity now; the passport can reuse its sections.
  • New data: the same find-at-unlock column as Märken 1; "Plats" only when the find has a location (location capture is opt-in).

D Exportera fältdagboken

The PDF export in 1.3 colours

The renderer (shared/pdf: JournalPdfMetrics plus one layout per platform) already uses the 1.3 colour values, but not the 1.3 look: every title is centred italic DM Serif, every other line is Caveat, the species list has a grey square where a photo should be, the badge page lists only the seven Premium badges, and the footer is "— 3 —". The pages below are A4, drawn from the renderer's own coordinates for "Now". The PDF is meant to be shared, so every plate photo carries its photographer and licence.

NowvC129

Cover, statistics, species

  • Five page types: title, statistics, species (25 per page, at most 50), badges (Premium only, if any), colophon.
  • No photos anywhere: a find is a grey square, a name and "Först: 2026-06-02". Your own photos are not used.
  • Free badges you earned (Nybörjare, Rödlistad and the rest) never appear in the export.
  • Layout bug: a full species page has 25 rows; row 25 sits at 806 pt and the page number at 812 pt, so they overlap (bottom of the third page).
PDF 1M1.3.1

The journal in 1.3: same pages, real photos

  • What changes: a cover with your most-seen bird as an untinted photo and the title below it ("Albins fältdagbok", the same genitive as Mina arter); statistics with the year ring you picked for the app; species in two columns with a photo per species; kickers with a rule, upright DM Serif with one rust italic word, Inter for facts, Caveat only for marginalia; footer "Birdy · Albins fältdagbok · 3".
  • Photos: your own photo of the species when you have one (no credit needed, marked "Eget foto"), otherwise the plate photo. The badge page lists every earned stamp, free and Premium. A last page, "Bildkällor", lists every plate photo used: file, photographer, licence and link, as CC BY and CC BY-SA ask.
  • Credit line per photo: under each plate thumbnail, "Foto: {author} · {licence}" in 6 pt; on the cover, a line under the photo. Author, licence and source URL are already in species.db (SpeciesImage). Cropped CC BY-SA photos are marked "beskuren".
  • Renderer work: load bitmaps (your photo from photoPath, plate photos from the asset pack) and draw them scaled down to about 300 px, JPEG-compressed, so 50 species stay near 3 MB; switch titles to upright DM Serif (already bundled); body in the platform sans, since Inter is not bundled; the same in JournalPdfLayoutIos.kt. Metrics stay shared, so both platforms keep matching.
PDF 2LLater

The plate book: two species per page

  • What changes: PDF 1, plus a page per two species like a field guide: a large photo, name and Latin name, first date, number of finds, its place in your life list, and your own note in Caveat. The cover becomes a four-photo grid of your most-seen birds. Stamps get their own page in their category inks.
  • Why: it is the export people would print or send to a parent. Your notes finally appear somewhere outside the app.
  • Cost: 50 species become about 25 pages and 5 to 7 MB; text wrapping for long notes on both platforms; a page-count cap or "only species with photos" setting. A week of work with QA.
  • Credits: one line under each plate photo; the four cover photos are credited under the grid.

E Premium efter några fynd

A Premium nudge at a proud moment, once

The app has two Premium moments today, both in EntryFlowDecider and AppScaffold: the day-0 Premium screen right after the intro, and a cold-start modal from day 7 that may come back every 3 days. Neither knows what the user has done. A sheet right after a save, once the journal has a few pages, asks at the moment the app has just proved itself.

The 3rd save is taken. InAppReviewTrigger asks for a Google Play review after the 3rd saved find, and the two must never stack. Recommendation: Premium after the 5th save and keep the review at the 3rd (early reviews matter more for a young app). Also, a user whose 5 finds are 5 species gets the "Nybörjare" unlock sheet on that same save, so the rule is "from the 5th save, on the first save where no other sheet is shown".

The moment

The 5th save, before the sheet

About a second later, the sheet slides up over this screen.

  • The find is already saved and the stamp has landed; nothing is interrupted.
  • The photo keeps its colours; the name sits on a neutral dark shade, not moss.
Pop-up 1S1.3.1

A paper card with the four true benefits

  • Content: only what Premium really adds (map, PDF, season statistics, 7 badges), the same list as the Premium screen; a line that scanning with photo and sound stays free. Prices come from Google Play; if they haven't loaded, the price line is simply left out.
  • "Se Premium" opens the existing Premium screen, which has the plan choice and the renewal disclosure, so the sheet never sells by itself. "Inte nu", the ✕ and a swipe down all close it, with no follow-up toast.
  • Effort: one composable and the trigger rules below, mostly reusing the Premium screen's strings.
Pop-up 2M1.3.1

Your own journal, previewed as a PDF

  • Content: two miniature pages drawn from the user's own finds (their photo or the plate photo, their name, their count), so the sheet shows something that is already theirs instead of a feature list.
  • Why: the PDF is the most tangible Premium feature, and the preview makes it concrete. Pairs naturally with PDF 1.
  • Effort: the preview is a small composable that mimics the PDF's cover and species page (not the real renderer, which runs on a background thread). Best after PDF 1, so the preview matches what you get.

When the sheet may appear

  1. Free users only. PremiumState must be Free. Early users are already Active(LIFETIME) through the grandfather override and Premium buyers are Active, so both are excluded by the same check the day-0 screen uses.
  2. Once per install. A new flag findsUpsellShown; after it is set the sheet never returns, whatever the user does.
  3. From the 5th saved find, on the first save where no other sheet is queued (badge unlock, notification permission, Play review).
  4. Not in the same session as the day-0 Premium screen, and not within 14 days of any other Premium prompt. A new lastUpsellShownAt is written by all three prompts and read by all three.
  5. Play-friendly: dismissible by ✕, "Inte nu", swipe and back; no countdown, no fake discount, no pre-selected purchase; prices only from Play with the period spelled out. The purchase itself happens on the Premium screen with its existing disclosure.
  6. Nothing tracked: the flag and timestamp live in DataStore on the phone, like the review trigger.

How it fits with what exists

Intro→Day-0 Premium screen (once)→3rd save: Play review (once)→5th save: this sheet (once)→Day 7+: cold-start modal
  • The cold-start modal currently returns every 3 days after day 7 for as long as the user stays free. With this sheet, suggest the shared 14-day rule for it too, so a free user sees at most one Premium prompt every two weeks.
  • Code: a new EntryFlowDecider.shouldShowFindsUpsell(count, state, shown, lastUpsellAt, day0ThisSession, otherSheetQueued), unit-tested like its neighbours, called from the same onObservationSaved hook as the review trigger.
  • Ship in 1.3.1, not 1.3.0: payments go live in 1.3.0. One or two weeks of day-0 conversion first gives a baseline to judge this sheet by.

F Introduktionen

A bolder intro: fewer scenes, real birds

Today's intro is eight scenes in a vertical pager: 0 language, 1 Birdy (the mascot), 2 photo, 3 sound, 4 the journal, 5 badges, 6 privacy, 7 name. Each scene is a headline, a sub-line and a small animated drawing. Only one real bird photo appears, inside a frame. Both options keep the language choice first, keep "Hoppa över" on every page and keep the replay from Inställningar.

NowvC129

Eight scenes, mostly text

  • Seven swipes before the app; the useful parts (what it identifies, that sound is free, that nothing leaves the phone) are spread thin.
  • Permissions are asked later and out of context: the camera at the first scan, the microphone at the first Lyssna, notifications after the first saved find.
  • Dagens fågel, the feature that brings people back, is not in the intro at all.
Intro 1M1.3.1

Five pages: language, a bird, a live demo, Dagens fågel, your name

  • Page 1 opens on a real bird, full width and untinted, with the promise below it. Page 2 is a live mini-demo: tap the photo and the scan brackets lock, the chip "Talgoxe" appears and stamp №1 lands; the Ljud tab plays the waveform and names the robin. Page 3 introduces Dagens fågel with today's real bird and a reminder switch. Page 4 merges name and privacy.
  • Permissions in context: notifications are asked on page 3 when you turn the reminder on (not after the first find). Camera and microphone stay at first use, which is what Android recommends. Location stays off until you turn it on in Inställningar.
  • What stays: the language page first, "Hoppa över", replay from Inställningar, the name defaulting to "Fältornitolog". Gone as their own pages: the mascot, journal, badges and privacy scenes (their lines move into pages 1, 2 and 4).
  • Data: Dagens fågel needs today's bird during the intro; DailyBirdTracker already computes it at start. The demo runs on a bundled photo with a canned result, no model call. Photos: Rödhake (Rob Hille, public domain), Talgoxe (Hobbyfotowiki, CC0), Lavskrika (Estormiz, CC0).
Intro 2LLater

Your first find is the intro

  • The idea: after language and one page with a bird, the intro hands you the real scanner: "Var är fågeln?" with camera, gallery and Lyssna. No bird nearby? "Prova med en exempelbild" runs the real photo model on a bundled CC0 photo and shows the real match screen, marked as an example and not saved.
  • Name and notifications move to the moment they mean something: the name is asked when your first real find is saved ("Första sidan"), the reminder after that.
  • Why: people learn by doing; the first stamp lands in the first minute.
  • Cost: an "example" mode through the match flow that never writes to the journal, the bundled photo (about 100 kB, Talgoxe, CC0), a first-save name sheet, and tests that the day-0 Premium screen and the notification ask don't collide with it. A week with QA.

Data the designs need that the app doesn't have yet

  • Which find earned a stamp (Märken 1b, Märken 2). BadgeUnlock stores only the badge id and the time. Add a nullable observation id written at unlock; for older stamps use the latest find saved before the unlock, and hide the row for stamps that came from the badge backfill.
  • Two DataStore values and a session flag for the Premium sheet: findsUpsellShown, lastUpsellShownAt, and "day-0 screen shown this session".
  • Bitmaps in the PDF renderer on both platforms (your photos from photoPath, plate photos from the asset pack). Credits already exist: author, licence and source URL per image in species.db.
  • A bundled example photo and an example mode that never saves (Intro 2 only).
  • Already there, only computed differently: the week's best find (red-list status and first sighting), life-list numbers, finds per weekday, the families and red-listed species you still lack, stamps per month and year, today's bird during the intro.
  • Strings only: rename the season badge "Året runt" to "Fyra årstider" (it clashes with the 12-month badge).
