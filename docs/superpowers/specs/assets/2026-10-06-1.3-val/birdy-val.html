Birdy 1.3.0 · design choices · 6 October 2026

Two screens to settle before 1.3.0.

You asked for a more appealing Season Statistics screen and for Dagens fågel to be easier to notice. For each one: the screen as it ships in vC129 today, then options drawn in the app's own palette and type, each with what changes, why it reads better, and what it costs.

Example data in every phone: 5 species, 15 observations from January to October 2026, Talgoxe seen most, May the busiest month, today is Tuesday 6 October. Not your real journal.

Effort: S is about a day including tests, SV + EN strings and new screenshots. M is two to three days. L is a week or more. Go-live is no later than 14 October.

Säsongsstatistik

Season statistics: a year you want to look at

Today the screen stacks two number boxes, twelve heavy bars, a four-color donut, a list and a line chart. With a few finds per month most bars reach the same height, the donut repeats the bars less precisely, and its winter navy is the one color outside the 1.3 palette. Both options keep the same data and differ in how much they redraw.

NowvC129

As it ships today

  • Totals sit in two boxes, then twelve equally heavy bars: with one or two finds a month, half the bars look the same and nothing stands out.
  • The donut says the bars again in four slices. Its winter navy is the only off-palette color, and brass means both "current month" (bars) and "summer" (donut), your open point (g).
  • Further down, Mest sedda is text with bars and Nya arter över tid is a line chart: correct, but no birds.

Calmer bars, seasons under the axis

  • One computed sentence in the header ("Din bästa månad var maj: 4 fynd") gives the screen a point before any chart.
  • Totals become one quiet row like Mina arter, with a new count: days in the field.
  • Thin bars with rounded tops on a faint baseline. Only the best month and the current month carry a number, and brass is used for the current month and nothing else.
  • Seasons move under the month axis as brackets with their sums, so the donut goes and the navy with it. Months are always one letter, so large text needs no switch.
  • Mest sedda gets each species' plate photo. Same data, same order of sections.

The year ring and a journal of firsts

  • The year is a ring that reads like a clock face: January at the top, so winter sits at the top and summer at the bottom, with the year's total in the middle.
  • Nya arter i år becomes a timeline of first sightings with photo and date, in place of the line chart: the same data, told as moments.
  • The top three species as photo seals. No donut, no navy, brass only for October.
  • Trade-off: a ring compares months less exactly than bars, so the best month and the current month carry their numbers.
  • Effort M: a new ring drawing, first-sighting dates per species (computed from saved finds), contrast and large-text checks, new screenshots.

Dagens fågel

Make Dagens fågel noticed

Today Dagens fågel lives in two places: the photo at the top of Identifiera and a text-only notification at 08:00. The photo already opens the species when tapped, but nothing says so. "Här just nu" shows for almost every bird, because the season data is "present" for every month. "0 / 3" counts the days you caught the daily bird toward the Premium badge Dagens fågel-jägare, which the screen never explains.

NowvC129

A photo you can't tell is a button

  • The whole photo is a tap target that opens the profile, with no button, arrow or hint.
  • "Här just nu" is the season line. With season data "present" all year, it reads the same every day.
  • "INTE FÅNGAD ÄN 0 / 3" is progress toward Dagens fågel-jägare (3 days, Premium) and says neither.
  • Only this tab knows about the daily bird. Mina arter, Uppslagsverk and the tab bar never mention it.

The same row after a save today.

  • Two visible actions: Läs om arten opens the profile, Lyssna efter den opens Lyssna (the existing audio ID).
  • The date in the kicker makes it a daily thing. The scientific name replaces "Här just nu", which never changes.
  • The progress row says what to do and what it counts: three seals for 0 of 3 days, a check and a filled seal after a save.
  • For free users the row can point to the free badge Första dagsfågeln instead of the Premium one. Your call.
  • Effort S: hero layout, a handful of new strings in SV + EN, screenshot tests.

Option A, plus it follows you through the app

  • Everything in Option A, plus a slim Dagens fågel strip on Mina arter (shown) and above the search field in Uppslagsverk. Tap opens the profile.
  • A rust dot on the Identifiera tab until today's bird has been opened, from any of the three places.
  • After a catch the strip shows "Fångad idag" with a filled seal instead of the nudge.
  • Effort M: a shared strip on two screens, a "seen today" date in preferences (Android, plus the iOS store so the Mac build keeps compiling), tests and screenshots.

The 08:00 notification, with the bird in it

  • Today: plain text, "Dagens fågel: Sävsångare / Här just nu.", at 08:00, on by default.
  • New: the species photo (bundled with the app, nothing downloaded), a line that invites a catch, and two buttons, Läs om arten and Lyssna efter den.
  • Reaches only people who allowed notifications. Android 13 and later asks once, as today.
  • Android only. The iOS notification is separate code and can follow later.
LaterEffort L · a week or moreAfter 1.3.0

A home-screen widget

  • Today's bird on the home screen. Tap opens the profile.
  • Needs Jetpack Glance (a new dependency), its own daily refresh, the photo and fonts in widget form, and testing on several launchers. iOS would need a separate WidgetKit extension in Swift.
  • Suggest it for 1.4, once you've seen whether the in-app changes move anything.
Considered, not drawn

Ideas left out, and why

  • Hör sången on the card. The app has no recordings per species. The species-pages pipeline fetches Commons recordings for the website; bundling them in the app means size, licensing per file and a player. L, later. Lyssna efter den is the honest version for now.
  • A real fact instead of the scientific name, such as how to recognise the bird. Possible once the species pages' checked facts exist; then it could feed the card, the strip and the notification.
  • A calendar heatmap of days in the field for the stats screen. Dense at phone width; could be added to either stats option later.
