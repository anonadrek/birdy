Learning birds by ear is hard. You hear something in a tree, and by the time you look up, the bird has gone quiet.

So we made a small listening exercise for every morning. It is a series of short videos, and it is called See the song.

> Listen first. The name comes next.

## How a video works

Each video is about 20 seconds long and made for your phone. It starts with the sound. The screen says *Sound on* and asks *Whose song is this?* While you listen, a ring of bars around a question mark moves with the sound. After a short pause, you see the bird's photo and its name.

For birds that are not songbirds, such as the Common Crane and the Mallard, the question is *Whose voice is this?* instead.

Here is the first one. Turn the sound on and try to guess before the name appears.

## A new bird every morning

The series started on Friday 9 October. For the first 30 days, a new video goes up every morning at 08:00 Swedish time, on Instagram, Facebook, YouTube Shorts and TikTok.

Here is the first week:

- Friday 9 October: [Eurasian Blue Tit](/species/eurasian-blue-tit/)
- Saturday 10 October: [Common Crane](/species/common-crane/)
- Sunday 11 October: [House Sparrow](/species/house-sparrow/)
- Monday 12 October: [Mallard](/species/mallard/)
- Tuesday 13 October: [Common Blackbird](/species/common-blackbird/)
- Wednesday 14 October: [Common Buzzard](/species/common-buzzard/)
- Thursday 15 October: [Great Tit](/species/great-tit/)

The text with each post is in English, with the bird's Swedish name in brackets. Each post has a link to that bird's page here on birdy.community, where you can play the same recording again and read more about the bird.

Missed a morning? Every video that has gone out so far is on the [clips page](/clips/).

## Open photos and recordings

The photos and recordings come from Wikimedia Commons, where people share their work under open licences: CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA. Every post names the photographer, the person who made the recording and the licence. When a video contains CC BY-SA material, the video itself is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.
